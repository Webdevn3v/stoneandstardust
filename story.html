The story behind it

Light, texture, movement.

Stone & Stardust creates handcrafted window pieces designed to become part of a room — catching natural light, adding reflective detail and bringing another layer of texture to the space.

What goes into a piece

Glass beads, crystal elements, natural stone accents, reflective pendants and mixed-metal details come together in combinations of color, shape and light. Materials vary from piece to piece, which is part of what keeps each one individual.

Window Veils

A Window Veil is the fuller styling idea: individual handcrafted strands layered intentionally across a window so the pieces read together as part of the room.

Window Accents + Suncatchers

Not every space calls for a full veil. Individual window accents and shorter suncatchers bring the same light-catching detail on a smaller scale.
