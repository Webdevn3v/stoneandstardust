Style your space

Different ways to live with the strands.

This page is about placement, not collections. Use a single strand as a small window accent, pair pieces for balance, layer several into a Window Veil, or drape them across the top of a window for a softer swagged effect.

Single Window Accent

One strand placed where the light catches it. Simple, quiet and easy to add without changing the whole room.

Balanced Pair

Two strands placed on opposite sides of a window for symmetry and a little more presence.

Window Veil

Several individual strands layered intentionally so they read together as one fuller window treatment.

Draped Window Swag

A strand or grouped strands draped across the top or corner of a window for a looser, more decorative line.

Suncatcher

A shorter hanging piece designed around light and movement — easy in smaller windows, corners and layered displays.

There is no one right way to hang them.

Length, spacing and layering can change with the window and the room. The custom request is there when a specific size, palette or placement needs to be worked out.
